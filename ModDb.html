Brass Brewery's Better Nutrition

 

"Remember that you don’t have to eat a perfect diet to be healthy. You will not suddenly get a nutrient deficiency, or gain weight from one snack, one meal, or one day of eating. It’s what you eat consistently over time that matters. Progress, not perfection, is what counts."

 

Brass Brewery's Hunger overhaul focuses on rewarding the player as they progress cooking technology with passive buffs over the long term course of the game while granting the player with diegetic feedback to their nutrition and food choices. All features are configurable and built in mind to allow users to configure the expierence to fit your playthrough. 


Features
  • Nutritional Buffs - Grants an array of passive buffs for boosting nutrition to multiple stats including Mining Speed, Melee Damage, Hunger Rate, Movement Speed. The base game Health modifier can be adjusted. A base modifier is available to adjust the natural starting stats to allow for a harder primitive age. See the table below for the default values. (See table for defaults)
  • Slow Starvation - Once below a percentage of satiety, start recieving passive debuffs while gaining reduced hunger that reduces as your hunger drops. (Default: 50% satiety, see table for all other defaults)
  • Passive Regeneration - Maintaining high saturation boost your natural regeneration scaling based upon your maximum health. Sitting while at high regeneration makes it even stronger. (Default when above 70% satiety, 0.25% HP/second or 1.25%HP/second when sitting)
  • Max Satiety Adjuster - Can change the max satiety of the player. (Default: 500)
  • Offhand Food Penalty - Vanilla penalizes the player with +20% hungerrate when holding an item in your offhand, this mod allow you to toggle this on and off. (Default: Offhand Hunger Penalty: false)
  • Starvation Damage - Allows changing the default starvation damage in vanilla, allowing the ability to remove the poultice starvation cheese or prevent slow long starvation deaths. (Default:  2.5 damage.  Vanilla is 0.125 damage)
  • Fully Configurable - Everything is configurable to allow it to be tuned to your desired playstyle
  • Modded Support - Should have few to no conflicting mods, and has built in enhancments with XSkills and Hydrate or Diedrate.

Mod Compatibility
 

Incompatibilities Warning

While there is no known incompatibilities at the current moment, please take caution and make backups of your world before adding new mods to your world.

Native Supported Mods

  • XSkills Fork - Option to grants xp boost for all nutritional stats. (Default: 3% per max nutrition type, up to 15%)
  • Hydrate Or Diedrate - Options to grants bonus cooling modifier and thirst rate multiplier for all nutritional stats (Default: 3% per max nutrion for both cooling modifier and thirst rate multiplier, to a total of 15% cooling modifier and -15% thirst rate)

Recommend Mods

  • Expanded Foods - Great for adding in new foods to help reaching high nutrition rates
  • Adventurer's Walking Stick - Great tool for getting more out of your food with tools to reduce your food drain long distance.
